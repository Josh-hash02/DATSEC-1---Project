5. Known limits

  • Fast hashing makes password guessing cheap. Plain SHA-256 can be brute-forced on GPUs. Use Argon2id, bcrypt or PBKDF2 with a salt for passwords.
  • Length extension. SHA-256(secret ‖ message) lets an attacker append data without knowing the secret. Use HMAC-SHA256 instead.
  • Hashing alone is not authentication. Anyone can hash a modified file, so the digest must come from a trusted channel or be signed.
